# Fudgy brownies

Makes 16 squares, in an 8in square pan.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup (115 g) butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/3 cup cocoa powder
- 1/2 cup plain flour
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp baking powder
- 3 oz dark chocolate, chopped

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 350°F and line the pan.
2. Melt the butter, then beat in the sugar, 2 eggs and the vanilla.
3. Fold in the dry ingredients and the chocolate.
4. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until just set in the middle.
